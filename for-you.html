Atlas / Who it's for

Made for students
competing for the
top spots.

Atlas isn't a general tool. It was built around the specific needs of students in finance, economics, and business who are serious about what comes after graduation.

"The top 1% of students don't have more talent. They have better information, better habits, and something working for them every single day."
Phillip Walsh · Builder of Atlas
Who gets real value from this

Three types of students. One advisor.

Finance / IB Track

Targeting investment banking or finance roles

Sophomore or junior. Recruiting cycle active. Tracking multiple applications while staying current on the market and prepping for technical interviews.

  • Morning brief covers your watchlist and sector moves before 8 AM
  • Interview drills in Goldman, JPMorgan, and Lazard formats
  • Cold email drafts that reference the firm's actual recent work
  • Application pipeline tracked by stage without a spreadsheet
Economics Major

Market-aware, building real fluency fast

You follow macro developments and Fed policy. You want to understand what's happening in markets, not just hear that "stocks were up."

  • Daily brief synthesizes macro news and market moves for your goals
  • Ask Atlas about any economic concept in plain language
  • News filtered by your interests, not a generic financial feed
  • Price alerts for the levels you're watching
Business / Consulting Track

Recruiting broadly across consulting and corporate

You have a wide funnel: consulting, rotational programs, corporate finance. You need case prep, market awareness, and a way to manage 15+ applications at once.

  • Case prep in McKinsey, Bain, and BCG formats
  • Application tracker handles a broad pipeline without losing anything
  • Contact log with follow-up dates so no relationship goes cold
  • Brief surfaces deadlines and overdue follow-ups every morning
You'll get the most out of Atlas if

This is for you.

  • ✓ You're actively recruiting for finance, IB, consulting, or business roles
  • ✓ You want to understand market moves, not just track a portfolio
  • ✓ You have multiple applications open and struggle to track them all
  • ✓ You want to prep for specific firm interviews, not generic "tell me about yourself" practice
  • ✓ You hate opening a new app to check something — you want it in your messages
Probably not the right fit if

This is not for you.

  • ✗ You want help with essays, research papers, or homework
  • ✗ You're looking for a general-purpose AI assistant
  • ✗ You have no interest in finance, markets, or professional recruiting
  • ✗ You're not on Telegram and won't be
