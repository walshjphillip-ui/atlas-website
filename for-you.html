type of student.

Atlas isn't trying to help everyone. It was designed around students competing for finance, consulting, and business roles at the highest level. If that's you, read on.

Student Profiles

The students Atlas is built around

📊 Finance / IB Track

Gunning for investment banking

Sophomore or junior. Summer analyst recruiting cycle. You're tracking multiple applications, prepping technicals, and trying to stay current on the market — all while taking 18 credits.

  • Morning brief covers your watchlist tickers and sector moves before 8 AM
  • Interview prep drills you on Goldman/JPM/Lazard formats specifically
  • Application tracker shows your pipeline by stage — who's at first round, who's at superday
  • Cold email drafts go to your Gmail before you've had coffee
📈 Economics Major

Economics student, market-obsessed

You're tracking macro developments, following Fed policy, and trying to build knowledge that makes you stand out in interviews and classroom discussions.

  • Daily brief synthesizes Fed news, macro moves, and economic data into one read
  • Ask Atlas about any economic concept — it explains, doesn't just link
  • Market alerts for key levels in equities, rates, or commodities
  • News filtered by your interests — not a generic financial feed
🏢 Business / Management

Business student targeting consulting or corporate

You're recruiting broadly — consulting, corporate finance, rotational programs. You need to stay current, prep for case interviews, and manage a wide funnel of applications.

  • Case prep tailored to McKinsey, Bain, BCG, or firm-specific formats
  • Application tracker manages 15+ opportunities without a spreadsheet
  • Networking CRM tracks every contact from career fairs and LinkedIn
  • Atlas flags follow-ups you've forgotten about before they go cold
⚖️ Pre-Law / Policy

Pre-law or policy track with finance exposure

You're tracking economic and regulatory developments. You need market awareness for law school applications, policy work, or finance-adjacent roles.

  • Morning brief covers regulatory news, SEC, Fed, and legislative developments
  • Behavioral interview prep for law firm and government positions
  • Atlas knows your goals and frames market news around policy implications
  • Application tracker works for law school apps and summer associate recruiting
Is this for me?

You'll get the most out of Atlas if…

Atlas is specialized, not general-purpose. It's at its best when you're actively recruiting, tracking opportunities, and trying to stay ahead of your peers.

  • ✓ You're in undergrad and actively recruiting for finance, IB, consulting, or business roles
  • ✓ You care about market movements and want to understand them — not just track a portfolio
  • ✓ You have multiple applications open at different stages and struggle to track them
  • ✓ You meet people at career fairs and networking events and want to actually follow up
  • ✓ You want to prep for specific firm interviews, not just generic "tell me about yourself" practice
  • ✓ You hate opening a new app just to check something — you want it in your messages

Probably not the right fit if…

Atlas is focused. That's intentional — but it means it's not trying to be your general homework assistant.

  • ✗ You want help with essays, research papers, or general coursework
  • ✗ You're looking for a general-purpose AI assistant — use ChatGPT for that
  • ✗ You have no interest in finance, markets, or professional recruiting
  • ✗ You want a social app or community — Atlas is one-on-one
  • ✗ You're not on Telegram and won't be — the whole experience lives there
