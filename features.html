The concept

Iron Man doesn't open a dashboard. He just talks to JARVIS.

The people with real advisors — private wealth managers, senior mentors, seasoned alumni — don't go looking for information. It comes to them. They get a brief in the morning. They get a heads-up when something changes. They have someone tracking their pipeline so nothing slips through.

That's what Atlas does. You don't manage it. You don't check it. You just wake up to a text, and when you need something, you ask.

Market Intelligence

It watches the market so you don't have to

Every morning before you wake up, Atlas sends you a brief built around your watchlist, the sectors you follow, and what's actually relevant to your career goals. Not generic market news — your market news.

Set price alerts on any ticker. Ask about any stock, sector, or index in real time. Atlas handles the monitoring; you handle the decisions.

  • Personalized daily brief with your watchlist tickers and percent moves
  • Live price data and sector summaries, any time you ask
  • Price alerts delivered as a text when your level is crossed
  • Breaking news explained in terms of what it means for you

Career Preparation

It preps you like a coach who knows the firm

Not generic interview questions. Firm-specific drill sessions — Goldman technicals, Blackstone case style, McKinsey behavioral format. Tell Atlas which firm and which round, and it runs you through it the way that firm actually interviews.

It tracks your applications, flags when a follow-up is overdue, and surfaces recruiting deadlines before they sneak up on you. Everything you'd want a recruiting advisor to handle — handled automatically.

  • Technical drills: DCF, LBO, comps, accounting, valuation
  • Behavioral questions in firm-specific format and tone
  • Market knowledge questions tied to current events
  • Application tracker — every stage, every firm, no spreadsheet
  • Morning brief surfaces deadlines and cold-contact follow-ups

Networking Outreach

It writes the email. You just hit send.

Tell Atlas who you want to reach and why. It pulls context on that person, their firm's recent work, and your background — then writes a personalized cold email that sounds like you.

If you've connected Gmail, the draft goes straight to your inbox. No copy-pasting, no reformatting. It's ready to send the moment you open it.

  • References the firm's actual recent deals and news
  • Written around your background — school, major, goals
  • Saved directly to Gmail drafts when you've connected your account
  • Works for cold outreach, informational interviews, and follow-ups
