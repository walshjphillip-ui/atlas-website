Atlas / Features

Current intelligence.
Future preparation.

Atlas keeps you informed on what's happening now and prepares you for what's coming. It runs in the background and texts you when it matters.

01 · Stay Current

Morning Brief

Every morning before you wake up, Atlas sends you a brief built around your goals, your watchlist, and what's relevant to your career track. Not generic news. Your news.

What's in every brief

  • Your watchlist tickers with live price and percent move
  • Market sectors you follow, with a line on what the move means for your goals
  • 3 news stories filtered by your career track and major
  • Active applications at first round or superday, flagged with context
  • Overdue follow-ups and reminders due in the next 48 hours
02 · Live Market Data

Market Data and Alerts

Ask about any stock, sector, or index and get live price data. Set price alerts on any ticker and Atlas texts you when it crosses the level you set.

What you can ask

  • Any stock price or percent move in real time
  • Sector summaries: market, tech, AI, energy, financials, crypto
  • Price alerts that fire as a text when your level is crossed
  • Add any ticker to your watchlist for daily coverage
03 · Get Ahead

Interview Prep

Live interview drills tailored to the specific firm and round you're preparing for. Not generic finance questions. Goldman first-round format, Blackstone case style, McKinsey behavioral structure.

Ask a question, get a model answer, then get graded on your response.

You: prep me for my Goldman first round

Atlas: Got it. Goldman IBD first round. Starting with technicals.

Walk me through a DCF from scratch. What are the three main components and what drives each one?

Prep that knows the firm

  • Technicals: DCF, LBO, comps, accounting, valuation
  • Behavioral questions in the format each firm expects
  • Market knowledge: sector trends, recent deals, macro conditions
  • Firm-specific: culture, "why us," recent news about the firm
  • Model answers for every question you get wrong
04 · Outreach

Cold Email Drafts

Tell Atlas who you want to reach and why. It drafts a personalized cold email and saves it directly to your Gmail drafts. You review, adjust if needed, and hit send.

You: draft a cold email to a VP at Lazard's restructuring group

Atlas: Done. Saved to your Gmail drafts. References their Enviva advisory role and connects it to your fixed income coursework. Want me to adjust the tone?

Emails that don't get deleted

  • Goes straight to your Gmail drafts, no copy-pasting
  • Written around your background: school, major, career goals
  • References the target firm's actual work and recent deals
  • Works for cold outreach, informational interview requests, and follow-ups
