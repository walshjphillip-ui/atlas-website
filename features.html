Your day, briefed before you wake up

Every morning before 8 AM, Atlas sends you a brief built around your goals, your watchlist, and what's relevant to your career track. Not generic news. Your news.

It surfaces what moved overnight, what to watch today, and what action items can't wait — including overdue follow-ups and recruiting deadlines.

  • Sectors you follow, with what each move means for your goals

03 · Interview Prep

Drilled in the format the firm actually uses

Not generic finance questions. Goldman first-round technicals. Blackstone case style. McKinsey behavioral structure. Tell Atlas which firm and round, and it runs you through it the way that firm actually interviews.

Ask any question, get a model answer in seconds. Concepts like DCF, LBO, comps, convexity, carry trade — explained in plain language whenever you need it.

  • Technicals: DCF, LBO, comps, accounting, valuation
  • Behavioral questions in firm-specific format and tone
  • Market knowledge questions tied to current events
  • Firm-specific context: culture, "why us," recent deals
  • Instant concept answers, in plain language, on demand

04 · Cold Email Drafts

Tell it who. It writes the email. You hit send.

Tell Atlas who you want to reach and why. It pulls context on the person, their firm's recent work, and your background — then drafts a personalized cold email that sounds like you.

If you've connected Gmail, the draft goes straight to your inbox. No copy-pasting. No reformatting. Open Gmail, review it, hit send.

  • References the firm's actual recent deals and news
  • Written around your background — school, major, career goals
  • Saved directly to Gmail drafts when you've connected your account
  • Works for cold outreach, informational interview requests, follow-ups
  • Atlas never sends — you review and send every email yourself

05 · Tracking & Reminders

Tell it what happened. It keeps the record.

No spreadsheet. No app to open. Tell Atlas you applied somewhere, met someone at a career fair, or got a first round — it tracks every application, contact, and deadline in your profile.

Connect Google Calendar (optional) and upcoming events surface in your morning brief automatically. Atlas remembers everything across conversations — every response gets more useful because it knows you better.

  • Application tracker: every firm, every stage, every deadline
  • Contact log with follow-up dates so no relationship goes cold
  • Reminders for deadlines, interviews, and follow-ups
  • Calendar awareness — events surface in your brief automatically
  • Memory that builds over time — your profile sharpens with use
  • Internship signals — relevant openings surfaced based on your goals
