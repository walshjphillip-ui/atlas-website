Everything Atlas
does for you.

Not a chatbot you go to. An advisor that runs in the background and texts you when you need to pay attention.

01 · Daily Intelligence
🌅

Morning Brief

Every morning before you wake up, Atlas assembles a brief built specifically around you — your watchlist, your applications, your upcoming reminders, and the market moves that matter for your specific goals.

It doesn't summarize everything. It summarizes what's relevant to you today.

📅 Tuesday, May 5

📈 Markets
SPY: $523.40 (+0.8%) — broad rally, risk-on
GS: $481.20 (+1.2%) — outperforming

📰 Top Stories
— Fed holds rates; Powell signals two cuts in H2
— Goldman upgrades energy sector outlook
— Private equity deal flow up 34% YoY

🎯 Your Move Today
1. Goldman SA round is Thursday — run an IB technicals drill
2. Follow up with Marcus Chen (Blackstone) — overdue 3 days

What goes into your brief

Atlas pulls live data from multiple sources and filters it through what it knows about you.

  • Market sectors you care about — with price and percentage move, not just "markets were up"
  • Your personal watchlist tickers with real-time quotes
  • News filtered by your major, career goals, and interests — not generic headlines
  • Active job applications at first-round or superday stage, with relevant context
  • Networking follow-ups that are overdue or due today
  • Reminders due within the next 48 hours surfaced at the top
  • Local business news if you've told Atlas your hometown
  • Specific action items — not "check LinkedIn," but actual tasks tied to your real situation
02 · Market Intelligence
📈

Live Market Data

Ask Atlas about any stock, sector, or market index and get live price data — not a generic explanation of what the stock market is.

Set price alerts on any ticker. Atlas texts you when the threshold is crossed, day or night.

What you can ask

Natural language. No commands to memorize.

  • "What's GS trading at right now?"
  • "How's the tech sector doing today?"
  • "Alert me when NVDA crosses $950"
  • "What's SPY's YTD performance?"
  • "How are energy stocks doing this week?"
  • Add any ticker to your watchlist — it appears in every morning brief
  • Sector summaries: market, tech, AI, energy, financials, crypto
03 · Career Preparation
🎯

Interview Prep

Atlas runs live interview drills tailored to the specific firm and round you're preparing for. Not generic finance questions — Goldman superday format, Blackstone case style, McKinsey behavioral structure.

Ask a question, get a model answer, then get graded on your response. Repeat until it's locked in.

You: prep me for my Goldman first round

Atlas: Got it — Goldman IBD first round. Starting with technicals.

Walk me through a DCF from scratch. What are the three main components and what drives each one?

Prep that knows the firm

Atlas knows the difference between a Goldman first round and a Blackstone superday. The questions match the format, the firm's reputation, and the level of the round.

  • Technical questions: DCF, LBO, comps, accounting, valuation concepts
  • Behavioral questions in the STAR format expected by each firm
  • Case questions for consulting and PE interviews
  • Market knowledge questions: sector trends, recent deals, macro conditions
  • Firm-specific: culture questions, "why us," recent news about the firm
  • Model answers for every question you get wrong
  • Session memory — it knows which round you have and what's at stake
04 · Outreach
✉️

Cold Email Drafts

Tell Atlas who you want to reach and why. It drafts a cold email personalized to your background and their firm — and saves it directly to your Gmail drafts folder.

You review, adjust if needed, and hit send. The draft is already there waiting.

You: draft a cold email to a VP at Lazard, I'm interested in their restructuring group

Atlas: Done — saved to your Gmail drafts. Subject: "Restructuring Interest — [Your Name], University of Tampa Economics."

The email references their recent advisory role on the Enviva restructuring and connects it to your fixed income coursework. Want me to adjust the tone?

Emails that don't get deleted

Generic cold emails get ignored. Atlas writes around what it knows about you — your school, your goals, your experiences — and references the actual firm.

  • Drafts go straight to Gmail — you never copy-paste anything
  • Personalized to your background: school, major, interests, career goals
  • References the target firm: recent deals, group focus, relevant news
  • Professional but not robotic — reads like a real person wrote it
  • Ask Atlas to revise the tone, length, or focus instantly
  • Works for networking emails, informational interview requests, follow-ups
