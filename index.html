Why Telegram

Why does Atlas run on Telegram instead of its own app?

Atlas texts you — you don't check Atlas

Every other AI tool waits for you to show up. Atlas flips that. Your morning brief arrives before you wake up. When NVDA crosses your alert price, you get a text. When a deadline is in two hours, Atlas reaches out. Most tools make you do the work. Atlas does the work and reports back.

What is Telegram, exactly?

Telegram is a messaging app used by over 900 million people — think WhatsApp or iMessage, but built with stronger privacy and not owned by Meta or Google. It's free, available on iPhone and Android, and takes 60 seconds to set up with your phone number. Atlas lives inside it the same way a contact does — it's just a conversation.

Is it secure?

Yes. Messages are transmitted over encrypted connections. Telegram doesn't read your messages or show you ads. Atlas only sees the messages you send directly to it — it has no access to your other Telegram conversations, your contacts, or anything else on your phone. Your Telegram account is entirely separate from your Atlas profile.

Don't have Telegram yet?

Download it free from the App Store or Google Play. Sign up with your phone number — same process as WhatsApp. Then tap the link above to open Atlas.
