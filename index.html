FAQ

Common questions.

Is Atlas free?

Yes — completely free during beta. No credit card, no trial period, no catch. Just open Telegram and start.

Do I need Telegram?

Yes. Atlas runs entirely inside Telegram, which means no app to download and no account to create beyond Telegram itself. It's available on iPhone, Android, and desktop.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is a general-purpose assistant with no memory of you. Atlas is built specifically for finance and business students — it knows your goals, tracks your stocks, sends you a daily brief, and takes real actions like finding internships and drafting emails. It's an advisor, not a chatbot.

What stocks and markets does it cover?

Atlas covers individual stocks (NVDA, PLTR, AAPL, etc.), major ETFs (SPY, QQQ, GLD), and sectors including tech, energy, finance, crypto, and AI. You can build a personal watchlist and get live prices anytime.

What data does Atlas store about me?

Atlas stores your profile (name, school, career goal, interests) and your conversation history to give better answers over time. Your data is never sold or shared with third parties. See our Privacy Policy.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes — Telegram works on iOS and Android. Atlas is designed for mobile first. Your morning brief lands like a text message.
