Common questions

Asked & answered.

Is Atlas free?
Yes — completely free during beta. No credit card, no trial, no catch.
Do I need Telegram?
Yes. Atlas runs entirely inside Telegram — no separate app or account beyond Telegram itself. It's available on iPhone, Android, and desktop.
How is this different from a general AI chatbot?
A general chatbot has no memory of you, no live market data, and no agency to act. Atlas is built for ambitious finance, econ, and business students — it knows your goals, tracks your stocks, runs your morning brief, surfaces real news as it breaks, and takes real actions like sourcing internships and drafting outreach.
What stocks and markets does it cover?
Equities (NVDA, PLTR, AAPL and the rest), major ETFs (SPY, QQQ, GLD, XLE), FX, treasuries, commodities, and sectors including tech, energy, financials, crypto, and AI. Build a watchlist and Atlas does the rest.
How fresh is the news and market data?
Live. Atlas pulls real-time prices and breaking news the moment they hit the wire — CPI prints, FOMC minutes, earnings, headlines — and ties them to the names on your watchlist.
What does Atlas store about me?
Your profile (name, school, goals, target firms, interests) and conversation history — used only to make answers sharper over time. Never sold, never shared. Full detail in the Privacy Policy.
