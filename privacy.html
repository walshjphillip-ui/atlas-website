Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Atlas ("we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

What we collect

  • Email address — when you sign up on this website, we store your email to track signups. We do not spam you.
  • Profile information — name, school, major, career goal, and interests that you provide during onboarding inside the Telegram bot.
  • Conversation history — your last 20 messages with Atlas, stored to provide context for better responses. This is automatically trimmed as you use the bot.
  • Usage data — anonymous page view analytics (via Vercel Analytics) to understand how people use the website.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect payment information (Atlas is free).
  • We do not track your location beyond what you voluntarily tell the bot.
  • We do not read your Telegram messages outside of your conversations with Atlas.

How we use your data

  • To personalize Atlas's responses to your goals and interests.
  • To deliver your morning brief at your chosen time.
  • To improve Atlas over time.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Your data is stored securely in Supabase (PostgreSQL). AI responses are processed via Anthropic's API — your messages may be sent to Anthropic's servers to generate responses, subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

Data deletion

You can delete your data at any time by messaging Atlas: "clear my memory" clears stored facts, and you can request full account deletion by contacting us.

Contact
